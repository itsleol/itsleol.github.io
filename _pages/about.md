<section class="home-section" id="about">
  <p class="home-section__label">About</p>
  <div class="home-section__content home-prose">
    <h2>Technology is social before it is technical.</h2>
    <p>My research focuses on how digital technologies shape daily life, social relationships, identity formation, and meaning-making. I am especially interested in how social inequalities are reproduced, challenged, or reinforced through the design and use of technology.</p>
    <p>Influenced by personal experiences and observations, I pay particular attention to marginalized communities, including women, LGBTQIA+ individuals, and rural populations, and I study how they navigate, negotiate, and reimagine technology in their own contexts.</p>
    <p>Since 2024, I have been a research assistant in the <a href="https://ai-and-society.org">AI & Society</a> Research Group led by <a href="http://www.im.pku.edu.cn/szll/dsjglyyyjys/20240520135748744032/index.htm">Dr. Pu Yan</a>. Under the mentorship of <a href="http://www.im.pku.edu.cn/szll/tsgfzyjs/lxk/index.htm">Dr. Xingkun Liang</a>, I have conducted fieldwork-based qualitative studies on rural women using short-video platforms and HIV-positive individuals navigating stigma and biographical disruption. I have also collaborated with <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=48Z5kYAAAAAJ&hl=en">Yunjie Tang</a> on studies of generative AI, gendered meanings, and LGBTQIA+ identity formation.</p>
  </div>
</section>
